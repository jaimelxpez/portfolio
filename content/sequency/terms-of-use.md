## 2. Service Description

Sequency is a daily math challenge application available on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) that allows users to:

* Solve a daily math sequence challenge
* Compete on daily, weekly, and global leaderboards
* Track personal streaks and scores
* Receive push notifications when new challenges are available

Each day, a new mathematical sequence challenge is generated. Users must identify the pattern and submit the correct answer. Scores are calculated based on difficulty, solve time, and number of attempts.
