## 7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:
* **Right of access:** Know what data we have about you.
* **Right to rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
* **Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"):** Request deletion of your data.
* **Right to object:** Object to the processing of your data.
* **Right to restriction:** Request that we limit the processing.
* **Right to data portability:** Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
* **Right to withdraw consent:** You can withdraw your consent for push notifications at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

### How to Exercise Your Rights

You can exercise any of these rights by sending an email to:
**[sequency@jaimelxpez.com](mailto:sequency@jaimelxpez.com)**

Include in your request:
* Your full name
* The email associated with your Sequency account
* The right you wish to exercise
* Any additional relevant information

We will respond to your request within a maximum of 30 days. If the complexity of the request requires it, this period may be extended by a further two months, in which case we will inform you within the first 30 days.

You also have the right to file a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)**: [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es), or with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all your data at any time from the "Profile" section of the app, selecting "Delete account".

When you delete your account:
* All your game data (scores, results, streaks) will be deleted from our servers
* Your leaderboard entries will be removed
* Your profile avatar will be deleted from storage
* Your Supabase Authentication account will be deleted

This process is **irreversible**.

> **Note:** Deletion of your account data from Sequency's servers does not affect data already processed by third-party providers (Firebase Crashlytics, etc.), which is subject to each provider's data retention policies as described in Section 6.

## 9. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:
* **Encryption in transit:** All communications use HTTPS/TLS.
* **Secure authentication:** Email/Password and Google Sign-In with session tokens managed by Supabase.
* **Database security:** Row Level Security (RLS) policies on all database tables ensure users can only access their own data.
* **Secure storage:** Avatar images are stored in protected storage buckets with access control policies.

## 10. Minimum Age

> **Sequency is intended for users aged 16 and over.**
>
> We do not intentionally collect data from minors under 16 years of age. If you become aware that a minor has provided personal data, please contact us to proceed with its deletion.

## 11. Leaderboard and Public Data

By using Sequency, you acknowledge that certain data is visible to other users through the leaderboard feature:
* Your display name
* Your profile picture (if provided)
* Your total score
* Your ranking position

If you do not wish this information to be visible, you may modify your display name in your profile settings or contact us to request removal from the leaderboard.

## 12. Push Notifications

Sequency may send push notifications to inform you about new daily challenges. You can manage your notification preferences at any time:
* **Android:** Settings > Apps > Sequency > Notifications
* **iOS:** Settings > Notifications > Sequency

Disabling notifications will not affect the functionality of the app.
