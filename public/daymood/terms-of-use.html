2. Service Description#

DayMood is an emotional journal application available on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) that allows users to:

  • Create and store personal diary entries
  • Receive automated emotional analysis using artificial intelligence
  • View emotional patterns and wellness statistics
  • Manage and export their personal data

The emotional analysis is based on the advanced model of Plutchik’s Wheel of Emotions and uses artificial intelligence technology (Google Gemini 2.5 Pro as the primary provider and OpenAI GPT-4o as backup, in accordance with their respective terms of service) to identify emotions, their intensity, and key factors.

IMPORTANT — AI Limitations and Mental Health Disclaimer:

DayMood is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide medical or psychological diagnoses, advice, or treatment. The emotional analysis provided by the AI is for personal self-awareness and guidance purposes only. The AI may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or inconsistent results. You should never rely on DayMood’s analysis as a substitute for professional psychological or medical advice.

If you experience mental health issues, depression, anxiety, suicidal thoughts, or any other condition, please consult a qualified mental health professional immediately.
